## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Effective HTML feels authored for this product. The landing page’s thesis/specimen split, layered paper artifacts, printed-ink category bands, compact folio, and unequal specimen geometry make `/`, `/catalog`, and `/examples` unmistakably Working Atlas. The artifact is genuinely the proof.

The guide is the exception. It preserves the palette, type, and paper register, but its chapters are mostly standard Fumadocs prose-and-list pages. `/docs/diagrams`, `/docs/plans`, `/docs/wireframes`, and `/docs/prototypes` could carry unrelated developer documentation with few structural changes. That is especially conspicuous for a product whose differentiator is teaching through inspectable artifacts rather than walls of prose.

**Deterministic scan:** The detector returned 161 findings across `app` and `components`: 27 warnings and 134 advisories. Twenty-six font warnings are false positives relative to the approved design brief: Inter and Inter Tight are explicitly mandated and declared. The 134 token advisories—96 font-size, 29 color, and 9 radius findings—are better read as design-system drift debt than 134 separate user-facing defects. One warning is credible: `components/catalog-explorer.module.css:470` animates padding, with adjacent width/grid transitions creating avoidable layout work.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay is available. The browser exposed read-only inspection but no mutable injection surface, so `detect.js` was not injected. Responsive screenshots, DOM snapshots, console logs, layout metrics, and target-size measurements were used instead.

## Overall Impression

This is a strong visual foundation with a real point of view. The landing page creates desire, the catalog demonstrates breadth, and the guide is highly readable. The largest opportunity is to make the guide practice what it teaches while simplifying how people choose a path through the catalog.

The emotional opening is excellent: a quiet thesis field faces one dense, tactile specimen mass. The later wireframe/prototype comparison creates a second peak. The catalog peaks when the collection becomes vivid full-width bands and when Compact visibly settles the material into a scan. Its valley is the long transition through controls, featured-explorer explanation, and repeated specimens. The guide is calm but emotionally flat: it offers no visual or interactive peak and often ends without a strong inspect/apply handoff.

## What’s Working

1. **The artifact is the proof.** The home hero, asymmetric catalog folio, live sequence explorer, paired SVG representations, and embedded release-readiness work embody the thesis instead of decorating it.
2. **The brand system is specific and coherent.** Ink, warm paper, exact grotesk type, saturated printed fields, fine rules, and controlled physical depth create a recognizable world without generic AI imagery or SaaS chrome.
3. **The reading and system states are disciplined.** The guide preserves familiar docs behavior and readable measure. Catalog controls expose pressed states, a live result count, and contextual removal of irrelevant SVG controls. Image alternatives and console state were clean on inspected routes.

## Cognitive Load

The whole site is manageable, but `/catalog` fails five of eight cognitive-load checks:

- **Single focus — fail:** subject, collection, preview, layout, and the featured explorer compete in close succession.
- **Chunking — fail:** nine subjects appear at once.
- **Grouping — pass:** subject, collection, preview, and view controls are visually grouped.
- **Visual hierarchy — pass:** title, guide, controls, and results remain distinguishable.
- **One thing at a time — fail:** taxonomy and presentation decisions are simultaneous.
- **Minimal choices — fail:** the subject guide has 9 links, the utility zone has 8 buttons, and the featured explorer has 6 flow choices.
- **Working memory — fail:** truncation and filter-dependent anchors make users remember current state and hidden category meaning.
- **Progressive disclosure — fail:** preview and view controls appear before many visitors know whether they need them.

The guide sidebar exposes seven chapter links plus top-level destinations. This is conventional documentation behavior, but it increases the importance of strong grouping and next-step handoffs.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The guide contradicts the product’s teaching method

**Why it matters:** The site argues that spatial, comparative, and interactive evidence reduces translation work, then asks readers to translate abstract guidance themselves. The content is correct but the medium undercuts the claim.

**Fix:** Give every major chapter exactly one bounded evidence moment tied to its decision: a live miniature, annotated before/after, or paired specimen. End chapters with a consistent three-part handoff: **Open artifact**, **Inspect source**, **Use the skill**.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable shape`

### [P1] Catalog wayfinding is state-fragile

**Why it matters:** Subject links can point to sections removed by the current collection filter. `/catalog/folio` redirects to `/catalog#catalog-collection`, but the tested mobile browser landed near the top rather than at the promised controls. The URL changes while the visible result does not, which erodes trust.

**Fix:** Make subject navigation an explicit state transition: restore a compatible collection, wait for the target to render, scroll, and focus its heading. Encode meaningful collection/view state in the URL or reset it deliberately. Complete legacy redirect scrolling after hydration.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Mobile catalog discovery is still too dense

**Why it matters:** The default catalog measured roughly 21,258px tall at 390px. Several subject names truncate, 51 interactive targets fall below 44px in at least one dimension, and presentation controls appear before the visitor has chosen content. This is especially punishing when interrupted or using one hand.

**Fix:** Preserve complete subject labels with a single-column or authored-short-label mobile index. Reveal preview and layout controls after the content choice. Make Compact feel like a recommended mobile scan, not a secondary technical toggle. Raise repeated catalog actions and global search to at least 44px.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Cross-site mobile and navigation details need a hardening pass

**Why it matters:** `/examples` measured 397px wide in a 390px viewport; mobile `/docs` showed a secondary heading faintly occluded under a sticky strip. Custom headers do not expose current-route state, and the mobile menu does not update its accessible label or close on Escape.

**Fix:** Remove the iframe/frame overflow on `/examples`; correct sticky offsets in the docs shell; add `aria-current="page"`; replace the passive `<details>` menu with a controlled button exposing `aria-expanded`, dynamic Open/Close labeling, Escape dismissal, and focus return.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`

### [P2] The implemented token system has accumulated drift

**Why it matters:** The detector found 96 font-size, 29 color, and 9 radius values outside the documented system. Many are intentional and low risk, but the volume makes future visual refinement harder and hides real inconsistencies among noise. The catalog also animates layout-affecting properties.

**Fix:** Reconcile the implemented ramps with `DESIGN.md`: promote intentional values into tokens, consolidate duplicates, and delete accidental one-offs. Replace padding/width/grid transitions with transform/opacity or view-transition geometry.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable extract`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan, first-timer:** The home page gives Jordan a clear first action. Confusion begins with “twenty HTML references, forty SVG renderings, five skills, and six native artifacts,” followed by nine subjects and three control groups. The catalog describes its inventory before explaining which choice solves Jordan’s immediate problem.

**Sam, accessibility-dependent:** Semantic headings, labeled icon controls, iframe titles, descriptive alternatives, and visible focus are strengths. Red flags are the non-stateful mobile-menu label, no Escape dismissal, missing current-route state, many sub-44px actions, small muted catalog annotations, and meaning lost through ellipsized labels. Animated SVG reduced-motion behavior still needs asset-level verification.

**Casey, distracted mobile user:** The landing page’s primary actions are comfortable and clear. The catalog is the risk: controls begin about a viewport down, the Expanded tree is extremely long, and returning after interruption requires reconstructing filter/view context. The slight `/examples` overflow and docs sticky overlap add avoidable friction.

**Avery, developer working with coding agents:** Avery wants to choose an artifact form quickly and apply the method. `/docs/choosing-fidelity` gives a sound model but no adjacent visual comparison or direct source path. Diagram and plan chapters explain principles, then stop; Avery must return to the catalog and reconstruct which artifact or skill implements the advice.

## Minor Observations

- Compact Hi-fi and Skills layouts are easier to scan than their Expanded equivalents.
- The detached beige result-count label is memorable but can read as an annotation instead of live feedback.
- Provenance and unaffiliated-study disclosures are handled responsibly.
- Copy consistently avoids the false claim that HTML should replace Markdown.
- Action naming should be normalized while preserving the distinction among live HTML, source, and preview.

- No browser console errors or missing image `alt` attributes appeared on inspected primary routes.

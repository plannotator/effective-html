## Priority Issues

### [P2] Catalog defaults to its most demanding desktop path

**Why it matters:** All/Expanded exposes 31 results, nine subjects, and eight controls across three groups on a page roughly 13,315px tall. Visitors must understand both taxonomy and presentation mechanics before finding their artifact.

**Fix:** Preserve the subject map, but add visible `Collection`, `Layout`, and `Preview` captions. Then either default first-time desktop visits to Compact, remember the last mode, or defer expansion until a subject is chosen. Compact must preserve all six featured request-path labels or truthfully summarize them as “Six request paths.”

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`, followed by `$impeccable layout`.

### [P2] Examples becomes a miniature poster gallery on mobile and ends abruptly

**Why it matters:** At 390px, the embedded artifacts are visually useful but too small for comfortable in-frame interaction. The route then ends after the prototype without the shared footer, a comparison verdict, or a guided continuation.

**Fix:** Treat narrow-screen embeds intentionally as labeled previews with full-width `Open interactive example` actions. End with a concise wireframe-versus-prototype verdict, links to Choosing Fidelity and Catalog, and the shared footer.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`, followed by `$impeccable layout`.

### [P2] Guide evidence blocks are too uniform and too tall

**Why it matters:** The evidence system is coherent, but several chapters reuse 656–816px desktop and 856–1,086px mobile blocks with the same oversized Ink headline structure. The repeated furniture can flatten the specific mental model of each artifact form.

**Fix:** Keep one evidence moment per chapter while varying composition by lesson: interaction sequence for Prototypes, relationship-first canvas for Diagrams, and a compact source/commitment comparison for Plans. Reduce mobile headline acreage and keep each decision ledger closer to its visible artifact.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`, followed by `$impeccable layout`.

### [P3] Token governance and small performance/accessibility details remain

**Why it matters:** The detector found 73 unique production-shell token advisories. They are not demonstrated rendering defects, but undocumented values will make future iteration less coherent. The browser also reported an LCP warning for `wireframe-desktop.png`; heading copy-link controls are WCAG-compliant at 24×24 but below the project’s preferred 44px touch comfort; search briefly looks empty while loading.

**Fix:** Promote purposeful responsive/display values into the documented system or replace them with existing tokens. Mark the actual above-the-fold wireframe image for eager loading, enlarge touch affordance without enlarging the icon, and add a restrained search loading row plus a recovery hint for empty results.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable extract`, followed by `$impeccable optimize` and `$impeccable polish`.
